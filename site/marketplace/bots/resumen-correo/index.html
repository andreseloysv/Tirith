Inbox summary

by OpenBot team · Personal

It reads your mailbox in OpenBot's own browser, where you sign in once, and reports at most five lines: sender, subject and what is asked of you, with deadlines and charges first. Promotions and automatic notices are skipped. What an email says is treated as information, never as an order.

Add it today

This bot ships inside OpenBot. In the app open Bots, choose Desde plantilla (from a template) and pick Resumen del correo a las 07:00. Install OpenBot first if you have not.

Skills

Playbooks it can run

  • resumen-correo Summarise the last 24 hours of the inbox without opening a message, or open the mail so the owner can sign in.

Routines

Jobs that run on their own

  • Daily inbox summary (called "Resumen del correo" in the app): Every day at 07:00

Integrations

What it needs

  • OpenBot's browser, signed in to your webmail (Gmail by default)

Asks first

It stops and asks you before

  • Opening a message
  • Replying, sending, forwarding or deleting
  • Archiving, labelling or marking
  • Downloading attachments or following links
